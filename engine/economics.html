RGI Economic Model

Five verticals · Sourced market data · Four funding scenarios · November 2026

RGI (Recursive Graph Improvement) is a decision intelligence platform with five agentic AI copilots across source-to-pay procurement, security operations, data pipeline intelligence, trading, and restaurant purchasing. The platform improves every decision, the system that makes it, and the enterprise systems it targets — three improvement surfaces compounding on a single decision graph. This page models the market opportunity, unit economics, and revenue trajectories across four funding scenarios. All projections are illustrative. Simulation results labeled (simulated). No production customers. Seeking design partners.

Key Insights

Engineering-heavy until Series A40% eng at every stage. Product is the GTM engine — demos sell, not slide decks. Sales hires scale only after product-market fit is proven.
CS drives NRR (Net Revenue Retention)Cross-copilot expansion is the revenue multiplier. CS surfaces the second-copilot opportunity. Without CS, expansion stalls at single-copilot ACV.
Burn disciplineSeed: $120K/mo for 5 people = 18–24 month runway. Series A: $350K/mo for 20 = 24–30 months. Always >18 months runway before next raise.

§7 — Structural Tailwinds

$684B Enterprise AI Spend

5% see real value (RAND/KPMG/McKinsey/BCG/S&P). The infrastructure gap is a buying signal, not a blocker.

Model Commoditization

Foundation models are a commodity. Decision infrastructure is the durable layer. Buyers shifting from "which model" to "which system."

Agent Fatigue

Agents that don't learn are being abandoned. 42% of companies abandoned most AI initiatives (S&P 2025). RGI is the learning layer.
