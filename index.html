Landscape

What each platform does well — and where they stop

Palantir AIP
Strong ontology, agent tooling, government-grade deployment
Agents are deployed statically. Don't evolve from production outcomes. No situation analysis — execute workflows, not reason.
Celonis
Process mining leader — shows WHERE bottlenecks exist
Can't tell you WHY. Can't fix it. Can't learn from the fix. Can't transfer the pattern. RGI adds the other four verbs.
SAP Joule
1,300+ skills, deep ERP integration within the SAP ecosystem
Ecosystem-locked outside SAP. Agents don't learn from execution. Can't reason across SAP and non-SAP systems.
Snowflake / Databricks
Data gravity. Strong read-path infrastructure and catalogs
Read-path, not write-path. Can store context. Can't make it compound. Ontology-based enrichment — static map, same depth every query.
Zycus Merlin
5 procurement agents, 80%+ touchless AP, Gartner MQ Leader 2026
Generation 2: agents execute, don't compound. Invoice #10,000 = invoice #1. No conservation law. When the category manager leaves, Merlin is unchanged.
Agent memory (Mem0, Zep, Letta)
Solve statefulness for LLM agents — what happened, what's true, how to act
Memory without judgment geometry. Store facts, not verified outcome-conditioned centroids. No conservation gate. No compounding curve.

They optimize models. RGI optimizes the decision infrastructure. Every platform above can dispatch, monitor, or catalog. None writes verified outcomes back into a decision graph that compounds. Day 1 they look the same. Day 90 they don't. The question isn't which platform is best on Day 1. It's which one is better on Day 90 than it was on Day 1.
