The problem

Restaurants run on a few points of margin — and they leak from every side

Food cost is the largest controllable line, in an industry that can't raise prices any further. The leak isn't one thing; it runs along eight structural fault-lines unique to perishable, demand-volatile, thin-margin food service — each answered by a pillar. Turnover and messy data, the engine handles.
