30-second summary — plays after you've seen the views

Automation assurance is the flagship of RGI Source-to-Pay, part of the Compounding Intelligence platform. The copilot sits across requisition, PO disposition, goods receipt, invoice matching, and payment — growing the share of PO spend it processes straight-through under a dollar ceiling and a monitored accuracy floor. When commodity prices shift, it pauses the affected spend categories back to your buyers until it relearns the new price landscape. Off-contract (maverick) spend is intercepted before it becomes an invoice exception; restricted-party and sanctions screening runs on every PO — the two costliest P2P failure modes that agentic workflows treat as separate tool calls.
