Score & Archive vs. Score & Learn

LangSmith runs your LLM-as-judge rubric and logs the scores. GAE takes the same scores, learns which criteria matter, and rewrites the rubric from verified outcomes.

What Score-and-Archive Can Never Do

  • NO TRAJECTORY 4.2/5 for six months, complaints rising. No curve — rubric can't see drift.
  • NO VERIFICATION Two agents, same score. One improving, one gaming the rubric. No way to tell.
  • NO TRANSFER Year tuning summarization evals. Now need RAG retrieval. Start from zero.
  • NO DISCOVERY Five criteria. Which predict satisfaction? No outcome feedback — you'll never know.
  • NO DEFENSE "Conciseness" gets 4.8. Users prefer the longer version. Rubric can't self-correct.
