Demo 3 · Volatility · Short-Vol Cliff · The Shape Every Vol-ETF Trader Knows

"The $17K Cliff Your Backtest Didn't See"

Context — the short-vol equity curve
Steady small gains. Rare deep losses. You're making a directional bet on volatility itself — long SVXY, short VXX, positions that profit when vol stays low. The equity curve climbs $500/week for 4 months. Then VIX spikes: −$18,000 in one day. Four months of work, gone. The health band fires AMBER two weeks before the cliff. That's the signal no backtest gives you — and 60–70% of funded-trader blow-ups end here (estimated from prop-firm drawdown data).

Run: two equity curves — same premium book, with vs without health band

What RGI shows that a backtest can't
  • • Health band fires AMBER before the cliff — that's the signal
  • • Without it: 4 months of $500/week, then −$18K in a day. Net: −$10K
  • • With it: AMBER at week 14 → step back → spike hits at −$2K, not −$18K → resume when vol is rich
  • • Net: +$7,200. SVXY recovers fastest in VIX 25–35 (78% accuracy, modeled)
  • • IV/RV chart below = supporting signal. Demo 8 formalizes for options (roadmap).

The trade-off: The health band fires conservatively. In this sample, it fired AMBER on 3 weeks that would have been profitable — missed gains of ~$1,500 (modeled). The cliff it caught: −$18,000. Net: +$16,500. The gate would rather cost you a small win than let you hold through a regime change.

Investor note: 60–70% of funded-trader blow-ups are drawdown breaches from short vol (estimated from prop-firm drawdown data). This is the shape that kills accounts — and the flag that fires before it.

Demo 8 · Premium Timing + VRP Term Structure · v1.1 Roadmap

"Sell Premium Only When It's Rich"

v1.1 · Roadmap · For volatility traders and investors
If your strategy is a bet on market volatility — selling premium, buying protection, timing VIX — this is the timing layer you don't have. Same strategy, split three ways: by IV/RV regime, by DTE, and by VRP state. The difference isn't the strategy — it's when you enter.
IV/RV < 1.2 (thin premium)
IV/RV > 1.5 (rich premium)
Same strategy. $600/trade difference (modeled). Your edge peaks at 7DTE when IV/RV > 1.5. The 0DTE volume is noise. GREEN only when premium is rich. v1.1 — on the roadmap.
The 0DTE trap: You already know 0DTE is noise — it's > 75% of retail SPX volume and retail lost ~$125M there. The VRP term structure shows where the real premium edge is: 7DTE, IV/RV > 1.5. That's a $600/trade difference just from timing. Most traders enter by feel. This is the math.
